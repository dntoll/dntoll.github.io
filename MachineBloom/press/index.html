One-line pitch

Machine Bloom is a tactical tower-defense game where every placement is shaped by a spatial puzzle—and every victory brings a machine-scarred world back to life.

Short description

Restore a ruined world through tactical tower defense and shifting shapes.

Long description

Machines have consumed the world. Build a defense network, break their advance, and let nature bloom again.

Machine Bloom combines classic tower defense with a spatial puzzle. Every tower uses the next pattern in your deployment sequence. Rotate the shape, find room on the grid, and place each weapon where it can protect the path.

Position laser, flame, and bomb towers against machines with different speeds, armor, and shields. Connect support towers along shared pattern edges to boost range, damage, or reload speed. Dense layouts are powerful, but careless clusters can drain your network.

Move towers when new routes open, match weapons to enemy defenses, choose targeting priorities, and decide whether an awkward pattern should be used now or skipped at a cost. Victories reclaim the landscape and award crystals for permanent upgrades.

Every placement is a weapon. Every shape changes the plan.

Key features

  • Shape-based construction turns every tower placement into a spatial puzzle.
  • Laser, flame, and bomb towers answer machines with different speeds, armor, and shields.
  • Shared edges create support networks that boost range, damage, or reload speed.
  • Moving towers, targeting priorities, and pattern skipping let the defense adapt as routes change.
  • Campaign victories restore the landscape and award crystals for permanent upgrades.

Studio bio

Curious Engineer Studio is an independent game studio based in Kalmar, Sweden. The studio creates tactical games where interconnected systems, meaningful decisions, and atmosphere matter as much as the fight. Machine Bloom: Tower Defense is the studio's current project.

Machine Bloom brings shape-driven tower-defense strategy to Google Play

Kalmar, Sweden — 20 August 2026

Independent developer Curious Engineer Studio today announced that Machine Bloom: Tower Defense is coming to Google Play. A closed playtest is currently in progress, giving invited players an early opportunity to test its distinctive combination of tactical tower defense and spatial construction.

In Machine Bloom, machines have consumed the world. Players build connected defense networks to stop their advance and help nature reclaim a scarred landscape. Every tower uses the next shape in a deployment sequence, turning each purchase into both a tactical choice and a spatial puzzle.

Laser, flame, and bomb towers answer enemies with different speeds, armor, and shields. Support towers become more effective when their shapes share edges with combat towers, but overly dense clusters risk draining the network. As routes change, players can move towers, adjust targeting priorities, invest in specialized upgrades, or skip an awkward shape at a cost.

Campaign victories restore the landscape and award crystals for permanent upgrades. Multiple difficulty levels let players refine their defense or face the machines at full strength.

Machine Bloom: Tower Defense is in closed playtesting for Android through Google Play. The release date will be announced later.

Machine Bloom tar formdriven tower defense-strategi till Google Play

Kalmar, Sverige – 20 augusti 2026

Den oberoende utvecklaren Curious Engineer Studio meddelar i dag att Machine Bloom: Tower Defense kommer till Google Play. Ett stängt speltest pågår och ger inbjudna spelare möjlighet att tidigt prova spelets särpräglade kombination av taktiskt tower defense och spatialt byggande.

I Machine Bloom har maskinerna slukat världen. Spelaren bygger sammankopplade försvarsnätverk för att stoppa deras framryckning och hjälpa naturen återta ett sargat landskap. Varje torn använder nästa form i en placeringsföljd, vilket gör varje köp till både ett taktiskt val och ett spatialt pussel.

Laser-, eld- och bombtorn möter fiender med olika hastighet, pansar och sköldar. Stödtorn blir effektivare när deras former delar kanter med stridstorn, men alltför täta kluster riskerar att dränera nätverket. När vägarna förändras kan spelaren flytta torn, justera målprioritering, investera i specialiserade uppgraderingar eller hoppa över en besvärlig form mot en kostnad.

Segrar i kampanjen återställer landskapet och ger kristaller till permanenta uppgraderingar. Flera svårighetsgrader låter spelaren finslipa sitt försvar eller möta maskinerna med full styrka.

Machine Bloom: Tower Defense speltestas för närvarande i ett stängt Android-test via Google Play. Releasedatum meddelas senare.
