One-line pitch

Machine Bloom is a tactical tower-defense game where every placement is shaped by a spatial puzzle—and every victory brings a machine-scarred world back to life.

Short description

Restore a ruined world through tactical tower defense and shifting shapes.

Long description

Machines have consumed the world. Build a defense network, break their advance, and let nature bloom again.

Machine Bloom combines classic tower defense with a spatial puzzle. Every tower uses the next pattern in your deployment sequence. Rotate the shape, find room on the grid, and place each weapon where it can protect the path.

Position laser, flame, and bomb towers against machines with different speeds, armor, and shields. Connect support towers along shared pattern edges to boost range, damage, or reload speed. Dense layouts are powerful, but careless clusters can drain your network.

Move towers when new routes open, match weapons to enemy defenses, choose targeting priorities, and decide whether an awkward pattern should be used now or skipped at a cost. Victories reclaim the landscape and award crystals for permanent upgrades.

Every placement is a weapon. Every shape changes the plan.

Key features

  • Shape-based construction turns every tower placement into a spatial puzzle.
  • Laser, flame, and bomb towers answer machines with different speeds, armor, and shields.
  • Shared edges create support networks that boost range, damage, or reload speed.
  • Moving towers, targeting priorities, and pattern skipping let the defense adapt as routes change.
  • Campaign victories restore the landscape and award crystals for permanent upgrades.

Successful Machine Bloom playtest leads to a new ambassador program

Kalmar, Sweden — 28 August 2026

Independent developer Curious Engineer Studio has successfully completed the 14-day closed playtest for Machine Bloom: Tower Defense. More than 20 testers took part and provided valuable feedback on the game's distinctive combination of tactical tower defense and spatial construction. More than 10 updates were released during the test as the game was refined in response to player experiences and reported issues.

With the closed test complete, the studio is now opening expressions of interest for a voluntary Machine Bloom ambassador program. Ambassadors can receive early access to features and updates, help test new versions when they have time, and offer honest—including critical—feedback as development continues toward a Google Play release.

In Machine Bloom, machines have consumed the world. Players build connected defense networks to stop their advance and help nature reclaim a scarred landscape. Every tower uses the next shape in a deployment sequence, turning each purchase into both a tactical choice and a spatial puzzle.

Laser, flame, and bomb towers answer enemies with different speeds, armor, and shields. Support towers become more effective when their shapes share edges with combat towers, but overly dense clusters risk draining the network. As routes change, players can move towers, adjust targeting priorities, invest in specialized upgrades, or skip an awkward shape at a cost.

Campaign victories restore the landscape and award crystals for permanent upgrades. Multiple difficulty levels let players refine their defense or face the machines at full strength.

The ambassador role has no fixed hours, activity targets, or requirement for positive feedback, public posts, ratings, or reviews. Android ambassadors may receive temporary test access to the full campaign through Google Play license testing. Ambassadors can also opt in to have a name or nickname included in the game credits.

With the closed test complete, Machine Bloom: Tower Defense is now being prepared for release on Google Play. The release date will be announced later.

Svenska strategispelet Machine Bloom: Tower Defense släpps på Google Play

Kalmar, Sverige – 10 september 2026

Machine Bloom: Tower Defense, ett taktiskt tower defense-spel där tornens former gör varje placering till ett pussel, finns nu tillgängligt för Android på Google Play. Spelet är utvecklat av Curious Engineer Studio i Kalmar.

I Machine Bloom har maskiner tagit över en ödelagd värld. Spelarens uppgift är att bygga ett försvar, stoppa maskinernas framryckning och låta naturen återvända.

Varje torn placeras med hjälp av en form som måste få plats på spelplanen. Formen kan roteras, men spelaren behöver hela tiden anpassa sitt försvar efter det tillgängliga utrymmet, fiendernas vägar och vilken form som står näst på tur. Torn som placeras intill varandra kan kopplas samman och förstärka varandra.

– Jag ville skapa ett tower defense-spel där placeringen av tornen är ett taktiskt beslut i sig. Det räcker inte att välja rätt torn – du behöver också hitta en plats där formen passar och där tornet kan samverka med resten av försvaret, säger Daniel Toll, utvecklare av Machine Bloom.

Spelet innehåller laser-, eld- och bombtorn samt flera typer av stödtorn. Variationen av fiender gör att spelaren behöver anpassa sitt försvar under stridens gång. Segrar återställer landskapet och ger kristaller som kan användas till permanenta uppgraderingar.

Inför lanseringen genomfördes ett två veckor långt stängt test med fler än 20 testare. Deras återkoppling bidrog till ett stort antal uppdateringar och förbättringar av spelet.

– Testarnas hjälp har varit ovärderlig. De har hittat problem, ifrågasatt sådant som varit otydligt och hjälpt mig att se spelet med nya ögon. Machine Bloom är ett bättre spel tack vare dem, säger Daniel Toll.

Machine Bloom: Tower Defense finns nu på Google Play. En version för iPhone planeras som nästa plattform, följd av en framtida Steam-version. Curious Engineer Studio planerar också en ytterligare kampanj med nya banor.
